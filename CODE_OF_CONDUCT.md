# Code of Conduct

RepoMind is built in the open by people who care about clear, useful tools. Keep it that way.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique the code, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers; there are no stupid questions.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any form, anywhere in the project.
- Disagree respectfully, then let the maintainer decide.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block anyone who doesn't follow this. Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub.

Adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
